Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining PennySurveys, completing surveys, and getting paid.

Joining PennySurveys is free and takes just a few minutes. Click the Get Started button, create your account, and complete your profile with accurate details. A full profile helps us match you with surveys that fit your interests and background.

Survey invitations depend on your profile, demographics, and the surveys currently available. Make sure your profile is complete and up to date, and check back regularly for new opportunities. We cannot send invites to incomplete or less-matched profiles.

Each survey has specific eligibility requirements such as location, age, industry, shopping habits, or product usage. If you do not match the target audience, you may be screened out before completing the survey. This is normal and helps keep the research accurate.

Rewards vary by survey length and complexity. Once your completed surveys are approved, the amount is added to your wallet. You can withdraw when your balance reaches the minimum payout threshold. We support secure payment methods such as M-Pesa, PayPal, and bank transfer.

Go to your dashboard and click Withdraw. Choose your preferred payment method, confirm your details, and submit the request. Processing time depends on the selected method and our payout schedule, but we aim to keep it as quick and transparent as possible.

Use the Forgot Password link on the login page to reset it securely. If you still need help, contact our support team at support@pennysurveys.com and we will be happy to assist with account, payout, or survey-related questions.
